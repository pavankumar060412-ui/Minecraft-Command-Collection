# Minecraft Beginner Survival Tutorial

A step-by-step guide to starting your first Minecraft survival world.

# Step 1: Starting Your World

When creating a new world:

Recommended settings:

```text
Gamemode: Survival
Difficulty: Normal
World Type: Default
```

Your first goal is to collect basic resources and create shelter.

---

# Step 2: Collect Basic Resources

Start by collecting:

## Wood

Break trees to get logs.

Use logs to craft:

- Planks
- Sticks
- Crafting Table
- Tools

---

## Stone

Mine stone using a wooden pickaxe.

Craft:

- Stone Pickaxe
- Stone Axe
- Stone Sword
- Furnace

---

# Step 3: Create Basic Tools

Important first tools:

## Wooden Pickaxe

Used to mine stone.

## Stone Pickaxe

Used to mine:

- Coal
- Iron
- Stone

## Stone Sword

Used for protection.

## Stone Axe

Used for collecting wood faster.

---

# Step 4: Find Food

Food sources:

- Animals
- Crops
- Villages
- Fishing

Good early foods:

- Cooked beef
- Cooked pork
- Bread
- Baked potatoes

---

# Step 5: Build A Shelter

Your first shelter should protect you from:

- Zombies
- Skeletons
- Creepers
- The night

Include:

- Walls
- Roof
- Door
- Torches
- Storage

---

# Step 6: Make A Furnace

Craft:

```text
8 Cobblestone
```

Use it for:

- Cooking food
- Smelting ores
- Creating materials

---

# Step 7: Find Coal Or Make Charcoal

Torches require:

```text
Coal + Stick
```

or:

```text
Charcoal + Stick
```

Torches prevent hostile mobs from spawning.

---

# Step 8: Start Mining

Mine for:

- Coal
- Iron
- Copper
- Redstone
- Diamonds

Important early resources:

## Iron

Used for:

- Armor
- Tools
- Shields
- Buckets

---

# Step 9: Make Iron Equipment

Recommended upgrades:

## Iron Pickaxe

Allows mining:

- Diamonds
- Redstone
- Gold

---

## Iron Armor

Provides protection.

Full set:

- Helmet
- Chestplate
- Leggings
- Boots

---

# Step 10: Create A Base

A good base includes:

- Storage room
- Farms
- Enchanting area
- Nether portal
- Animal pens

---

# Step 11: Prepare For The Nether

Before entering the Nether:

Bring:

- Iron armor
- Shield
- Food
- Blocks
- Water bucket
- Pickaxe

Create:

```text
Obsidian Portal
```

---

# Step 12: Upgrade Your Gear

Goals:

- Diamond tools
- Diamond armor
- Enchantments
- Netherite equipment

---

# Useful Early Game Goals

Complete:

- Full iron armor
- Diamond tools
- Enchanting table
- Nether portal
- Villager trading setup

---

# Beginner Tips

- Always carry food.
- Never dig straight down.
- Light up caves.
- Store valuable items safely.
- Keep backups of important worlds.
- Carry blocks for emergencies.
