# Minecraft Farming Tutorial

A complete guide to creating farms, growing resources, and building automated systems.

# What Is Farming?

Farming is the process of producing renewable resources in Minecraft.

Farms provide:

- Food
- Materials
- Trading items
- Experience
- Building resources

---

# Basic Farming Requirements

Most farms need:

- Water
- Light
- Space
- Seeds/items
- Proper conditions

# Creating A Crop Farm

Steps:

1. Find a flat area.
2. Create farmland using a hoe.
3. Place water nearby.
4. Plant seeds.
5. Add lighting.
6. Harvest when grown.
