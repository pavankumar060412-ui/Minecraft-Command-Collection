# Minecraft Advanced Survival Tutorial

A guide to progressing from basic survival into advanced Minecraft gameplay.

# What Is Advanced Survival?

Advanced survival focuses on:

- Efficient resource gathering
- Automation
- Powerful equipment
- Large projects
- Long-term world development
