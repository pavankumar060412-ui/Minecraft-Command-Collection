# Minecraft Netherite Tutorial

A complete guide to finding, crafting, and using Netherite equipment.

# What Is Netherite?

Netherite is the strongest material in Minecraft.

It is used to upgrade diamond equipment into powerful Netherite gear.

Benefits:

- Higher durability
- Better performance
- Fire resistance for dropped items
- Stronger equipment

---

# Netherite Items

Netherite can create:

- Netherite Sword
- Netherite Pickaxe
- Netherite Axe
- Netherite Shovel
- Netherite Hoe
- Netherite Armor

---

# Materials Needed

To create Netherite equipment:

You need:

```text
Diamond Equipment
+
Netherite Upgrade Smithing Template
+
Netherite Ingot
```

---

# Finding Ancient Debris

Ancient Debris is found in:

```text
The Nether
```

Best mining level:

```text
Y Level 15
```

---

# Ancient Debris Mining Methods

## Branch Mining

Method:

- Dig tunnels
- Search surrounding blocks
- Avoid lava

---

## TNT Mining

Uses explosions to clear areas.

Advantages:

- Fast
- Reveals hidden debris

---

## Bed Mining

Beds explode in the Nether.

Warning:

```text
Beds explode in the Nether.
```

Use carefully.

---

# Smelting Ancient Debris

Ancient Debris becomes:

```text
Ancient Debris
        ↓
Netherite Scrap
```

Using:

```text
Furnace
or
Blast Furnace
```

---

# Crafting Netherite Ingots

Recipe:

```text
4 Netherite Scrap
+
4 Gold Ingots
=
1 Netherite Ingot
```

---

# Smithing Netherite Equipment

Required:

- Smithing Table
- Diamond item
- Netherite Upgrade Template
- Netherite Ingot

Process:

```text
Diamond Gear
      ↓
Smithing Table
      ↓
Netherite Gear
```


---

# Netherite Tips

- Carry Fire Resistance potions.
- Mine carefully around lava.
- Store ancient debris safely.
- Use Fortune on other ores, not ancient debris.
- Upgrade important gear first.

---

# Netherite Progression

Recommended order:

1. Netherite Pickaxe
2. Netherite Armor
3. Netherite Sword
4. Netherite Tools
5. Extra equipment
