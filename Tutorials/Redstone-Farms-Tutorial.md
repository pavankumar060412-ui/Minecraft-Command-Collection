# Minecraft Redstone Farms Tutorial

A guide to building automated farms using redstone systems.

# What Are Redstone Farms?

Redstone farms use Minecraft mechanics and redstone components to automatically collect resources.

They use:

- Pistons
- Observers
- Hoppers
- Villagers
- Water systems
- Redstone circuits

---

# Basic Redstone Farm Components

## Observer

Detects block changes.

Used for:

- Crop growth detection
- Automatic machines

---

## Piston

Moves blocks.

Used for:

- Breaking crops
- Moving structures

---

## Hopper

Collects and transfers items.

Used for:

- Automatic collection
- Storage systems

---

## Chest

Stores collected items.

---

# Automatic Sugar Cane Farm

Produces:

- Sugar cane
- Paper

Required:

- Sugar cane
- Water
- Sand/dirt
- Observer
- Piston
- Hopper

Working:

```text
Sugar Cane Grows
       ↓
Observer Detects Growth
       ↓
Piston Breaks Sugar Cane
       ↓
Hopper Collects Items
```

# Storage Systems

Connect farms to:

- Hoppers
- Chests
- Sorting systems

Example:

```text

     ↓
Hopper
     ↓
Item Sorter
     ↓
Storage
```
