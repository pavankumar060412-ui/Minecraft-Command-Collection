# Minecraft End Dimension Tutorial

A complete guide to reaching, surviving, and exploring the End dimension.

# What Is The End?

The End is the final major dimension in Minecraft.

It contains:

- Ender Dragon
- End Cities
- Shulkers
- Elytra
- Valuable loot

---

# Preparing For The End

Before entering the End, prepare:

## Armor

Recommended:

```text
Diamond Armor
or
Netherite Armor
```

Enchantments:

```text
Protection IV
Unbreaking III
Mending
```

---

## Weapons

Bring:

- Sword
- Bow
- Pickaxe
- Shield

Recommended:

```text
Sharpness V
Power V
Unbreaking III
Mending
```

---

## Supplies

Bring:

- Food
- Blocks
- Water Bucket
- Ender Pearls
- Golden Apples
- Potions

---

# Finding The Stronghold

To find a stronghold:

1. Obtain Ender Pearls.
2. Obtain Blaze Powder.
3. Create Eyes of Ender.
4. Throw them to follow the direction.
5. Locate the stronghold.

---

# Activating The End Portal

The End Portal requires:

```text
12 Eyes of Ender
```

Place them into the portal frame.

---

# Entering The End

When entering:

You will find:

- Obsidian platform
- Ender Dragon
- Endermen
- End crystals

---

# Fighting The Ender Dragon

## Step 1: Destroy End Crystals

End crystals heal the dragon.

Destroy them using:

- Bow
- Blocks
- Careful climbing

---

## Step 2: Avoid Dragon Attacks

Avoid:

- Dragon breath
- Knockback attacks
- Falling damage

---

## Step 3: Attack The Dragon

Best time:

When the dragon lands on the portal.

Use:

- Sword
- Axe

---

# Ender Dragon Drops

The dragon drops:

- Experience
- Dragon Egg
- Access to End gateways

---

# End Gateways

After defeating the dragon:

End gateways appear.

They lead to:

- Outer End islands

---

# Exploring Outer End Islands

Outer islands contain:

- End Cities
- Chorus plants
- Shulkers

---

# End City

End Cities contain:

- Valuable loot
- Diamond gear
- Enchanted items

---

# Shulkers

Shulkers are enemies found in End Cities.

They drop:

```text
Shulker Shells
```

Used to craft:

```text
Shulker Boxes
```

---

# Elytra

Elytra are found in:

```text
End Ship
```

They allow:

- Gliding
- Flying with fireworks

---

# Firework Flight

Use:

```text
Firework Rockets
```

to boost Elytra flight.

---

# End Farming

Important End farms:

## Enderman Farm

Provides:

- Experience
- Ender Pearls

---

# Respawning The Ender Dragon

Required:

```text
4 End Crystals
```

Place one on each side of the exit portal.

---

# End Survival Tips

- Bring lots of blocks.
- Use slow falling potions.
- Carry a water bucket.
- Do not look at Endermen.
- Protect yourself from falling.

---

# Advanced End Goals

Complete:

- Defeat Ender Dragon
- Get Elytra
- Collect Shulker Boxes
- Build Enderman Farm
- Explore End Cities
