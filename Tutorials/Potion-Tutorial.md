# Minecraft Potion Tutorial

A complete guide to brewing and using potions in Minecraft.

# What Are Potions?

Potions are items that give players special effects.

They can provide:

- Strength
- Speed
- Healing
- Protection
- Special abilities

---

# Brewing Basics

To make potions you need:

- Brewing Stand
- Blaze Powder
- Water Bottles
- Nether Wart
- Potion Ingredients

---

# Brewing Stand

Crafting recipe:

```text
1 Blaze Rod
3 Cobblestone
```

The brewing stand requires:

```text
Blaze Powder as fuel
```

---

# Basic Brewing Process

Steps:

1. Fill glass bottles with water.
2. Place bottles in brewing stand.
3. Add Nether Wart.
4. Add ingredients.
5. Collect the potion.

---

# Awkward Potion

# Splash Potions

Splash potions can be thrown.

Created using:

```text
Potion
+
Gunpowder
```

Useful for:

- Multiplayer
- Combat
- Healing teammates

---

# Lingering Potions

Lingering potions create an area effect.

Created using:

```text
Splash Potion
+
Dragon's Breath
```
