# Minecraft Farming Guide

A complete guide to farming systems in Minecraft, from basic crops to advanced automatic farms.

# What Is Farming?

Farming is the process of producing renewable resources such as food, materials, and items.

Farms are useful for:

- Food supply
- Resource collection
- Trading
- Automation
- Survival progression

# Farming Mechanics

## Farmland

Farmland is created using:

```mcfunction
/hoe
```

or a hoe tool on dirt/grass blocks.

---

## Water Range

One water source can hydrate farmland up to:

```text
4 blocks in every direction
```

---

## Light Requirements

Most crops require:

```text
Light level 9 or higher
```

# Farming Tips

- Build farms near your base.
- Use villagers for efficient farming.
- Add storage systems with hoppers.
- Use automatic farms for renewable resources.
- Light up areas to prevent mob problems.
