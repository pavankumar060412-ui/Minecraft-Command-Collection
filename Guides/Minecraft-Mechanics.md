# Minecraft Mechanics Guide

A complete guide to important Minecraft mechanics, systems, and hidden features.

# What Are Minecraft Mechanics?

Minecraft mechanics are the rules and systems that control how the game works.

They include:

- Movement
- Combat
- Mobs
- Blocks
- Redstone
- World generation
- Game physics

---

# World Mechanics

## Day And Night Cycle

Minecraft has a repeating cycle:

```text
Day: 10 minutes
Night: 10 minutes
Full Cycle: 20 minutes
```

---

## Difficulty

Difficulty affects:

- Mob damage
- Hunger
- Mob spawning
- Natural regeneration

Modes:

```text
Peaceful
Easy
Normal
Hard
```

---

# Block Mechanics

## Gravity Blocks

Blocks affected by gravity:

- Sand
- Red Sand
- Gravel
- Concrete Powder
- Anvils

---

## Water Mechanics

Water can:

- Flow
- Push entities
- Hydrate farmland
- Create farms

Water flows:

```text
Up to 8 blocks
```

---

## Lava Mechanics

Lava:

- Burns items
- Damages players
- Creates stone and obsidian

# Combat Mechanics

## Critical Hits

Critical hits happen when:

- Falling while attacking
- Not sprinting

They deal extra damage.

---

## Attack Cooldown

Java Edition uses attack cooldown.

A fully charged attack deals maximum damage.
