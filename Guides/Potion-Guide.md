# Minecraft Potion Guide

A complete guide to brewing potions, ingredients, effects, and useful potion combinations.

# What Are Potions?

Potions are items that give players or mobs special effects.

They can provide:

- Speed
- Strength
- Healing
- Protection
- Night vision
- Special abilities

---

# Brewing Basics

To create potions you need:

- Brewing Stand
- Blaze Powder
- Water Bottles
- Nether Wart
- Ingredients

---

# Brewing Stand

Crafting recipe:

```text
3 Cobblestone
1 Blaze Rod
```

The brewing stand uses:

```text
Fuel: Blaze Powder
```

---

# Basic Brewing Process

1. Fill glass bottles with water.
2. Place bottles in brewing stand.
3. Add Nether Wart.
4. Add the desired ingredient.
5. Add modifiers if needed.

---

# Potion Ingredients

## Nether Wart

Creates:

```text
Awkward Potion
```

Used as the base for most potions.

---

## Blaze Powder

Used as:

- Brewing fuel
- Strength potion ingredient

---

## Redstone Dust

Effect:

- Increases duration

---

## Glowstone Dust

Effect:

- Increases power

---

## Gunpowder

Creates:

```text
Splash Potions
```

Allows throwing potions.

---

## Dragon's Breath

Creates:

```text
Lingering Potions
```

Creates a cloud effect.

# Potion Modifiers

## Extended Duration

Use:

```text
Redstone Dust
```

Example:

```text
3:00 → 8:00
```

---

## Increased Strength

Use:

```text
Glowstone Dust
```

Example:

```text
Strength I → Strength II
```

---

## Splash Potion

Use:

```text
Gunpowder
```

Thrown at targets.

---

## Lingering Potion

Use:

```text
Dragon's Breath
```

Creates an area effect.

# Potion Tips

- Always carry Fire Resistance before exploring the Nether.
- Use Redstone for longer adventures.
- Use Glowstone for stronger effects.
- Store potions in your inventory before dangerous fights.
- Brewing is easier with a steady Blaze Powder supply.
