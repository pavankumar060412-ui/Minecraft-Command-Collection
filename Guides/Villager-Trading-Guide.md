# Minecraft Villager Trading Guide

A complete guide to villager jobs, trading systems, professions, and getting the best deals.

# What Are Villagers?

Villagers are passive Minecraft mobs that can trade items with players.

They are useful for:

- Getting rare items
- Enchantments
- Emerald farming
- Resource trading
- Building economies

---

# Villager Basics

Villagers need:

- A bed
- A workstation
- Food
- A village area

---

# Villager Trading Hall

A trading hall contains:

- Villager booths
- Workstations
- Beds
- Storage

Benefits:

- Easy access to trades
- Organized villagers
- Better trading

---

# Getting Specific Enchantments

For librarians:

1. Place a lectern.
2. Check the first trade.
3. If unwanted, break the lectern.
4. Place it again.
5. Repeat until the desired book appears.

---

# Villager Level System

Villagers have levels:

```text
Novice
Apprentice
Journeyman
Expert
Master
```

Trading increases their level.

---

# Zombie Villager Discounts

A zombie villager can be cured using:

- Splash Potion of Weakness
- Golden Apple

Benefits:

- Lower prices
- Better deals

---

# Villager Breeding

Villagers breed when:

- Beds are available
- They have enough food
- Conditions are met

Food:

- Bread
- Carrots
- Potatoes
- Beetroot

---

# Trading Tips

- Lock good trades before replacing villagers.
- Keep valuable villagers safe.
- Use trading halls for efficiency.
- Cure villagers for discounts.
- Stock emerald farms.
